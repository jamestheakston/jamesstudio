Introducing Pillar Group

A new chapter for our digital ventures. A holding company built for longevity, utility, and restraint.

Photo by Iona Pippin on Unsplash

Today, we're excited to announce Pillar Group—a digital holding company that will own, operate, and support our focused technology businesses. This represents a new chapter in how we structure and grow our digital ventures.

Why a Holding Company?

As we've grown from a single web development practice into multiple technology products, we needed a structure that supports focused, autonomous businesses while maintaining shared values and operational principles. Pillar Group provides that structure.

Each company within the group operates autonomously with its own product roadmap, engineering team, and customer commitments. But they all share a common philosophy: utility over noise, craft over scale, and respect for the person using the software.

Our Operating Brands

Pillar Group currently owns and operates two active brands:

James Studio

A UK-based web development and design practice building minimalist, high-performance websites and full-stack applications. This is where it all started—our original web agency.

Classcastle

A teacher web app paired with a lightweight browser extension for students. One click, and thirty screens land in the same place. Free for teachers, works on Chromebooks and Firefox.

Our Principles

Every brand under Pillar Group follows three core principles:

  • Deliberate Engineering: Fast load times, clean architecture, and zero bloat. We treat technical maintainability as a core product feature.
  • Strict Data Boundaries: User data is never monetized or cross-pollinated for advertising. Trust is maintained through structural separation and minimal data collection.
  • Quiet Simplicity: Software should get out of the way. We strip away unnecessary friction so people can finish their work and move on with their day.

Data Governance

We take data privacy seriously. If you use multiple services owned by Pillar Group, your name may be transferred between these services strictly for account identification purposes. No other personal data—including your email address, billing details, usage history, or account credentials—is shared between Pillar Group services.

Each operating brand maintains its own independent infrastructure and data handling practices. For complete details regarding data retention, processing, and your statutory rights, consult the dedicated Privacy Policy published on the specific service you use.

What This Means for You

If you're a client of James Studio, nothing changes operationally. You'll continue to work with the same team, receive the same high-quality service, and benefit from our commitment to excellence. The formation of Pillar Group simply provides a better structure for us to build and support multiple technology products going forward.
