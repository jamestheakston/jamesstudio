02 / PRINCIPLES

Utility over noise. Craft over scale.

We build software that respects the person using it. Rather than chasing short-term growth metrics, we focus on durable engineering, clear interfaces, and straightforward business models.

I.

Deliberate Engineering

Fast load times, clean architecture, and zero bloat. We treat technical maintainability as a core product feature rather than an afterthought.

II.

Strict Data Boundaries

User data is never monetized or cross-pollinated for advertising. Trust is maintained through structural separation and minimal data collection.

III.

Quiet Simplicity

Software should get out of the way. We strip away unnecessary friction so people can finish their work and move on with their day.

03 / DATA GOVERNANCE

Cross-Service Privacy

How account information is isolated across Pillar Group entities.

If you use multiple services owned by Pillar Group, your name may be transferred between these services strictly for account identification purposes.

No other personal data—including your email address, billing details, usage history, or account credentials—is shared between Pillar Group services. Each operating brand maintains its own independent infrastructure and data handling practices.

For complete details regarding data retention, processing, and your statutory rights, consult the dedicated Privacy Policy published on the specific service you use.
