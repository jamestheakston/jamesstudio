Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 23rd September 2026

At James Studio, we take your privacy seriously. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website or use our services.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us through our contact form, including your name, email address, and project brief details. We only collect this information when you voluntarily submit it to us.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to your project inquiries, provide development framework estimates, and communicate with you about potential collaborations. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Project Inspection: We may use keyword detection to inspect and analyze your project briefs to better understand your requirements and provide more accurate estimates. This is a local detection process—your email address is never sent anywhere and remains private. This processing is done solely for the purpose of improving our service to you and is not used for any other purpose.

Cookies and Analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to improve your experience on our website:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the website to function properly, including navigation and access to secure areas.
  • Analytics Cookies: We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors interact with our website. This helps us improve our content and user experience. Microsoft Clarity collects anonymous data about your browsing behavior, including pages visited, time spent on pages, and click patterns.

You can control your cookie preferences through our cookie banner, which appears on your first visit. You can also change your cookie settings at any time by clicking "Cookie Settings" in the footer.

Microsoft Clarity

We use Microsoft Clarity, a web analytics service provided by Microsoft Corporation. Microsoft Clarity uses cookies and similar technologies to collect information about your use of our website, including:

  • Pages you visit and how long you spend on each page
  • Your browser type and operating system
  • Referring website information
  • Click patterns and user interactions

This information is used to analyze trends, administer the site, track users' movements around the site, and gather demographic information. Microsoft Clarity does not collect personally identifiable information. For more information about Microsoft Clarity's privacy practices, please visit Microsoft's Privacy Policy.

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt-out of analytics cookies at any time

Security and Bot Protection

We use Cloudflare Turnstile to protect our contact form from spam and automated abuse. Turnstile is a security service that helps verify that submissions are made by real users rather than bots.

How Turnstile Works: When you submit our contact form, Turnstile may perform security checks in the background. This includes analyzing your browser behavior, IP address, and other signals to determine if you're a legitimate user. Turnstile does not collect personally identifiable information beyond what is necessary for security verification.

Custom Worker: We use a custom Cloudflare Worker to process contact form submissions. This worker handles Turnstile validation on our servers and sends form submissions via email. All form data is processed securely and your information is never stored on third-party servers beyond what is necessary for email delivery.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Cloudflare Turnstile: For bot protection and security verification on our contact form
  • Cloudflare Workers: For processing contact form submissions securely
  • Resend: For sending email notifications from our contact form
  • Microsoft Clarity: For website analytics
  • Supabase: For database storage of quote callbacks
  • Puter: For cloud computing services (not used for contact forms)

These services may collect data according to their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review their privacy policies for more information.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at legal@jamesstudio.uk for privacy concerns, through our contact form, or at our GitHub repository.

What is the Client Portal? The James Studio Client Portal is a secure dashboard that allows our clients to directly edit their website content without needing to contact our team. When we create a website for you, we set up a GitHub repository for your project. You get access to that repository in the dashboard, where you can use a visual editor to make text and image changes. When you click "Publish", your changes are committed to the repository and automatically pushed to your live website. The portal also includes AI-powered editing assistance (optional) to help with content modifications.

At James Studio, we take your privacy seriously. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Client Portal.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you sign in to the Client Portal, including your email address and authentication credentials. We do not collect any GitHub credentials or require you to connect your GitHub account.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide access to the Client Dashboard, authenticate your account, and enable you to edit your website content. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

GitHub Integration: We handle all GitHub integration on our backend. You do not need to connect your GitHub account or provide any GitHub credentials. We only access the repositories you have been granted permission to edit, and we use this to load your website content into our visual editor and save changes back to your repository.

Third-Party Services

The Client Portal uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: For user authentication and access control management
  • GitHub API: For accessing and editing your repository content
  • Puter: For AI-powered content editing assistance (optional feature)
  • Monaco Editor: For code editing functionality

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information and repository content. We handle all GitHub integration securely on our backend, and you never need to provide GitHub credentials. All changes to your repositories are made through GitHub's API with our authorization.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your account information
  • Request deletion of your account
  • Request removal of repository access
  • Opt-out of AI features (Puter)

Beta Service

The Client Portal is currently in beta. We may make changes to features and functionality as we improve the service. Your feedback is valuable to us.

Disclaimer: As a beta service, features may break unexpectedly without warning. We take no liability for any issues, data loss, or service interruptions that may occur while using the Client Portal. We recommend backing up your data regularly.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices regarding the Client Portal, please contact us at legal@jamesstudio.uk for privacy concerns.
